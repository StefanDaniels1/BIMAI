## Shared rules, personal teams

Everyone on a project shares its agreements, decisions and knowledge. Each person has their own
**seat**: their team, tasks, journal and outputs.

## Positions: jobs, not people

A **position** is a job on the project, for example `modeller-structure` or `bim-coordinator`.
People hold positions. What the agents learn in a job stays with the position, so a colleague who takes
over starts with everything that was done.

- **More modellers or coordinators?** Add positions split by discipline, zone or model.
- **Someone ill or on holiday?** `bimai cover modeller-structure --by bob --until 2026-10-14`. Work
  goes to Bob and switches back automatically.
- **Someone leaves?** `bimai transfer modeller-structure --to fatima`. Fatima inherits the job.

## Every responsibility has one owner

Each responsibility belongs to exactly one position. Other teams can read it but not act on it. When an
agent finds work in someone else's area, it **hands it over**: the work appears in that person's tasks
with the evidence attached.

## Nobody loses context

After every session a short journal entry is written: what was asked, what was done, decisions and
handoffs, and what's still open. Ask "what did the team do this week on the viaduct?" and you get the
answer with links. The journal describes work, never time spent.

```bash
bimai graph project      # all positions, who holds them, each person's team, open handoffs
```
