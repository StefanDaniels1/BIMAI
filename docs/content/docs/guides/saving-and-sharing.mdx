---
title: Saving and sharing
description: bimai saves your team's work and shares it with your colleagues, without git commands.
---

<Status state="available">Works in any project that is shared through git (GitHub, Azure DevOps, GitLab, …). You never type a git command.</Status>

## It happens by itself

- **When you start a session**, bimai first brings in your colleagues' work, then your seat brief says what's
  new: *"Updated from the team: 3 changes."*
- **At the end of each answer**, bimai saves what your team produced and shares it in the background:
  decisions, handoffs, learnings, setup and the reports in `outputs/`.

You can also ask for it: *"Save and share my work"* (or run `bimai sync`).

## What is shared, and what isn't

| Shared by bimai | Never touched by bimai |
|---|---|
| bimai's project files: decisions, handoffs, positions and learnings, seats, team setup | your models and drawings (`.rvt`, `.ifc`, `.esa`, `.dwg`, …) |
| the team's reports in `outputs/` | your own notes and other files |
| `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/agents/`, `.mcp.json`, the shared settings | files over 25 MB (bimai names them, so you can share them yourself) |

Your session log and personal settings stay on your computer.

## Nothing is ever lost

- **Different files:** the normal case. Everyone has their own seat, and decisions and handoffs are one file each,
  so two people working at the same time almost never touch the same file.
- **The same part of the same file:** bimai stops the update, **keeps your version** (saved), and tells you which
  file and who else changed it. Sort it out together; nothing was overwritten.
- **Offline:** your work is saved on your computer and shared the next time.
- **No shared repository yet:** bimai saves on your computer and says so.

bimai never force-pushes, resets or rewrites history, and your team's agents aren't allowed to either: the
project's shared settings block those git commands for them.

## Settings

Switch the automatic saving and sharing off for a project in `.bimai/project.yaml`:

```yaml
sync:
  auto: false
```

Then share with `bimai sync` when you want.

```bash
bimai save    # save the team's work on this computer
bimai sync    # save, bring in the team's work, share yours
```
