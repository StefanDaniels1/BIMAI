The Planner lives on your **desk**, the private part of bimai that spans all your projects.

## Tasks come in three ways

1. **You type them:** "remind me to check the IFC export for Bridge 3 before Thursday."
2. **From meetings:** actions assigned to you in a processed transcript.
3. **From tools:** for example ACC issues assigned to you.

Tasks for a project are stored in that project, so your team can see what you're working on. Tasks
that belong to no project stay on your desk.

## Plans

- **Morning plan** on workdays: today's plan, overdue items and this week's deadlines.
- **Friday review:** what got done, what slipped, what's next.
- **Deadline warnings** 48 hours before something is due.

With a calendar connection, the plan uses your real free time; otherwise your working hours.

```bash
bimai tasks due --days 7
bimai tasks plan --day
```
