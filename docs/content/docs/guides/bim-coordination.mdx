## Issue triage

`/bimai:triage` reads the open ACC issues, clusters related ones, and flags overdue and unassigned
issues. It proposes assignments; nothing changes in ACC until you approve.

## Coordination meetings

`/bimai:meeting-prep` builds the agenda from open issues, model checks and open decisions. After the
meeting, `/bimai:meeting` turns the transcript into minutes and tasks (see [Meetings to tasks](/docs/guides/meetings)).

## Delivery checks

`/bimai:delivery-check` checks a model against the delivery checklist and the BEP agreements (naming,
properties, classification, coordinates) and reports pass or fail per rule, with the elements involved.

## Models without opening Revit

Through Autodesk's AEC Data Model, agents can read cloud models, quantities and differences between
versions without Revit running. That also makes these checks usable in scheduled runs.
