title: Files and folders

---

## On your computer

```
~/bimai/
├── desk/                      private to you, backed up to your own remote
│   ├── projects.yaml          your projects
│   ├── tasks.yaml             tasks that belong to no project
│   ├── plans/                 day and week plans
│   └── preferences.yaml       working hours, language
└── projects/
    └── a2-knooppunt/          a project, shared with its team
```

## In a project

```
.bimai/
├── project.yaml               project settings, bimai version, language, write mode
├── agreements.yaml            agreements from the BEP, with page references
├── conventions.md             house rules every agent follows
├── ownership.yaml             positions, scopes, who holds and covers them
├── people.yaml                project members and external parties
├── workflows/  automations/   the project's workflows and schedules
├── context/                   BEP, EIR, checklists (edited by people only)
├── data/                      Relatics and planning exports (raw files not shared by default)
├── decisions/                 one file per decision
├── meetings/                  minutes per meeting (raw transcripts not shared by default)
├── positions/<id>/            per job: what the agents learned, incoming handoffs
├── seats/<name>/              per person: team, tasks, journal, runs
└── outputs/<name>/            documents produced for each person
```

## Who sees what

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| Rules, agreements, decisions, positions, minutes | Everyone on the project |
| Your team, project tasks, journal and outputs | Everyone on the project |
| Raw exports and transcripts | Only your computer, unless the project opts in |
| Your desk | Only you |
| Passwords and tokens | Nobody, not even the agents |
