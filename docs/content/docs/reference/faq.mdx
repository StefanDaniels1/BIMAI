title: FAQ
description: Short answers to common questions.
---

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Do I need to know how to program?">
No. You talk to your team in plain language. bimai handles git, files and setup. VS Code is only the
window you work in; a Mentor can join your team to explain it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can bimai change my model?">
Only after you approve the exact change. New projects start read-only, and scheduled runs never change
anything.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does it work on a Mac?">
Yes, except the connections to Windows-only programs (Revit, Civil 3D, OpenRoads, Navisworks). Cloud
connections, files, meetings, planning and reports work everywhere.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where is our project data stored?">
In your own project repositories on your own git host, and on your own computers. Raw exports and
meeting transcripts stay local by default.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What if a colleague is ill?">
Someone covers their position with one command. Their tasks and incoming work go to the person
covering, who can read the journal to see where things stood.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Which AI does it use?">
Claude, through Claude Code, under your organization's account. Most work runs on the smaller, cheaper
models.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is it really free?">
bimai is open source under the MIT license. You pay for AI usage and your existing software licences.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
