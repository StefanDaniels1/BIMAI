A colleague started a bimai project and you'll work in it too, for example as constructeur next to the BIM
coordinator. Copy this prompt into Claude Code, in the project folder (or anywhere: Claude asks where the
project is):

<JoinPrompt />

## What happens

1. **Claude installs bimai** and gets the project: give it the link your colleague sent (`bimai join <link>` puts
   it in your Documents/bimai folder).
2. **It asks a few questions** as clickable choices: your name, your role, what you want help with and your
   tools. The project's settings are already there, so there's no project setup.
3. **You get a position**: your job on the project, such as `constructeur` or `bim-modeller`. If someone
   already has that job, you choose: a position of your own (another zone or discipline) or sharing theirs.
4. **Claude shows your team** and who else is on the project, and only adds you after your yes.

Nothing of your colleagues' setup changes: everyone has their own seat, and the files you share are the same
for everybody.

## Every session after that

When you open Claude Code, your **seat brief** tells your team who you are and what's new for you since last
time: handoffs from colleagues, new decisions, and (if your project shares it) what the team did. Just ask
*"What's new for me?"* See [Working together](/docs/guides/working-together).

## Doing it yourself

```bash
bimai join https://github.com/your-org/viaduct   # get the project
bimai join --interview                       # the questions, as JSON
bimai join --person Joris --role constructeur --tools scia --dry-run
bimai join --person Joris --role constructeur --tools scia --yes
```
